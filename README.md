# 🎭 MASKING CO. LLC
### Your smile is part of the job.

A webcam-driven psychological horror game where your real facial expressions determine whether you survive.

---

## 🩸 Overview

**MASKING CO. LLC** is a psychological horror game set in a modern corporate world where emotions are no longer personal — they are tools.

In this society, people wear emotions like masks to function.

At work, you must smile.  
With strangers, you must stay neutral.  
With management, you must remain pleasant.

The company provides the mask.

The company watches.

And the company knows when you stop performing.

Using real-time webcam face tracking, your **actual facial expressions become the game controller**.

If you fail to maintain the correct emotion, consequences follow.

---

## 🏢 Narrative Setting

Welcome to **MASKING CO. LLC**,  
a company specializing in “Emotional Compliance Solutions.”

Employees are required to:

- maintain a pleasant expression
- regulate emotional output
- wear assigned masks
- comply with surveillance systems

The handbook states:

> “Emotions are part of your uniform.”

But something feels wrong.

Sometimes the lights flicker.  
Sometimes shapes move in the dark.  
Sometimes the mask won’t come off.

And sometimes…  
you forget what your real face looks like.

---

## 🎮 Core Concept

### Face as Controller

This game does not use traditional inputs.

Instead:

Your face → Webcam → Emotion Detection → Gameplay

You don’t press buttons.

You perform.

---

## 🔁 Core Gameplay Loop

1. Explore the environment
2. Switch between mask states
3. Maintain required emotions
4. Avoid or confront threats
5. Survive your shift

Tension comes from:

- being constantly watched
- forced smiling
- emotional fatigue
- limited perception
- uncertainty about what is real

---

## 🎭 Mask System

The world exists in two perception layers.

### Mask On
- safer
- limited vision
- filtered reality
- hides threats

### Mask Off
- reveals truth
- exposes hidden paths
- increases danger
- enemies see you clearly

Players must strategically switch between concealment and exposure.

---

## 😊 Emotion System

Your webcam tracks facial expressions in real time.

Different emotions directly affect gameplay.

| Expression | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Smile | Lights on, safer zones, enemies retreat |
| Neutral | Normal state |
| Fear | Hidden threats appear |
| Anger | Short burst of power |
| Sadness | Sanity drains |
| No Face / Looking Away | System penalty |

Emotions are not cosmetic.

They are mechanics.

---

## 🧠 Emotional Labor Mechanic

The game simulates emotional exhaustion.

Holding a forced smile too long may cause:

- tracking instability
- blurred vision
- hallucinations
- mistakes

You are not just managing monsters.

You are managing yourself.

## 🎥 Emotion Detection System

Real-time facial tracking pipeline:

Camera  
→ Face Landmarks  
→ Feature Extraction  
→ Emotion Classification  
→ Gameplay State Machine  

Tracked features include:

- mouth width / smile intensity
- eye openness
- eyebrow tension
- head pose
- facial movement

Output:

Smile: 0.82
Fear: 0.10
Anger: 0.05
Sad: 0.03


These values directly control gameplay systems.
